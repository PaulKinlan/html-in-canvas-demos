Native popovers anchored to controls on a tilted panel

The panel is HTML that a canvas draws on a quad in perspective. Its buttons open browser-native top-layer UI with no script: Scenes opens a popover through commandfor, ? shows a popover="hint" through interestfor (hover, keyboard focus, or a long press on touch), and Turn off opens a modal <dialog>. The popovers are placed with CSS anchor positioning against buttons that are only pictures on a texture. Move the two sliders on the panel, or switch on the swing, and an open popover follows its button.

  1. The markup. commandfor, command and interestfor on buttons inside the canvas, pointing at elements outside it.
  2. The anchors. anchor-name on a button in the canvas, position-anchor on its popover.
  3. The panel's matrix. One matrix draws the quad and, through updateElementGeometry(), tells the browser where the panel is. Anchors and hit tests use that place.
  4. On paint. Upload the panel when it changed, draw its shadow and the panel.
  5. The fragment shader. A highlight that depends on the tilt, and a ring around the button whose popover is open.

How it works

The panel is one drawable child of the canvas, uploaded as a texture and drawn on a quad through a matrix with perspective. The same matrix goes to updateElementGeometry() in each paint event. From then on the browser's hit tests, focus rings and anchor rectangles for the panel's buttons use the drawn place. The popovers and the dialog are ordinary elements outside the canvas; commandfor and interestfor open them and CSS anchor positioning places them. The canvas repaints every frame only while the panel swings or a popover is open.

Findings

  • In Chromium 157 the anchor rectangle of an element inside a drawn canvas child is the bounding box of its drawn quad. With a 50 degree turn and perspective, a 60 by 30 pixel button gave an anchor of 34 by 29 pixels at the projected place, and a popover with position-area: bottom sat under it.
  • An open popover moves with its button when the panel's matrix changes. The page calls updateElementGeometry() in each paint event and does nothing else for the popover.
  • commandfor with toggle-popover, hide-popover and show-modal works from a button drawn in perspective, by mouse, touch and keyboard. The target does not have to be in the canvas.
  • The range inputs on the panel can be dragged while the panel turns under the pointer, because each new matrix moves the hit box as well.
  • The popover's arrow flips with an anchored(fallback: flip-block) container query when position-try-fallbacks moves the popover above the button.
  • Chromium 153 keeps the anchor at the element's layout box, so there the popovers open at the panel's untransformed place. Chrome 159 on the phone is not verified.
  • The popovers and the dialog are drawn by the browser above the canvas. The canvas cannot draw over them or bend them.
