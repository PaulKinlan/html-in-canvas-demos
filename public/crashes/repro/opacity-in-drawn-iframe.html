Opacity around a drawable canvas, inside an iframe that is drawn into a canvas, crashes the tab

This page draws a same-origin iframe into a canvas with drawElementImage(). The framed document has its own drawable canvas inside a box. With button A that box has opacity: .02. With button B it has visibility: hidden.

Expected: the frame is drawn.
Seen: headless Chromium 157, this page, 2026-11-21: A crashes the tab. B does not.
