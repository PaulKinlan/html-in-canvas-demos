loseContext() on a drawable WebGL canvas that holds an element texture crashes the tab

A drawable WebGL canvas uploads its child with texElementSubImage2D(). Then WEBGL_lose_context.loseContext() is called. Button A leaves the canvas in the page. Button B removes it in the same task. Button C does B with an iframe child in the canvas.

Expected: a webglcontextlost event.
Seen: headless Chromium 157: tab crashes in fuller pages (/protected/, /selection/, /player-fx/, /iframe-tiles/). A reduced page did not crash in 12 runs. What this page does is in the entry on /crashes/.
