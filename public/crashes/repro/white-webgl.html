Animated transparent WebGL 2 canvas with drawable children

Runs a 3 s frame loop on a transparent WebGL 2 drawable canvas uploading 6 mutating HTML chips via texElementSubImage2D() and drawing textured quads. Samples a plain 2D copy once per second to detect all-white or all-blank frames and watches webglcontextlost.

Expected: crisp orange chips moving over the dark page background.
Seen: Chrome 159 on Android turned the transparent WebGL canvas of /particles/ solid white with no webglcontextlost event.
