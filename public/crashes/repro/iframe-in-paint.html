Inserting or removing an iframe inside a paint handler crashes the tab

A drawable canvas with a paint listener. Button A appends an iframe to the canvas inside the listener. Button B removes an iframe that is a child of the canvas inside the listener. Button C appends one from a setTimeout(0) started in the listener.

Expected: the iframe is inserted or removed.
Seen: headless Chromium 157, this page, 2026-11-21: B (remove) crashes the tab. A (insert) and C do not crash here. In fuller pages an insert or a move inside paint crashed with the same CHECK (trap int3).
