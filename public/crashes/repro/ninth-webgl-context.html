A ninth WebGL context in a tab takes the oldest one away

The page makes WebGL contexts one at a time, on canvases that stay in the page, up to 17. This is a documented limit (8 per tab on Android, 16 on desktop) and not a bug. It is listed because it was the first cause of lost canvases on the site.

Expected: on Android, webglcontextlost on canvas 1 when canvas 9 gets its context.
Seen: Chrome on Android: the oldest context is lost at the ninth. Desktop: at the seventeenth.
