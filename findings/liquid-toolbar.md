# Liquid glass floating toolbar (`/liquid-toolbar/`)

Measured in headless Chrome 160.0.8188.0 canary (`C160`) on 2026-11-27 at `412x915` mobile (`deviceScaleFactor = 2.625`, SwiftShader WebGL) and `1040x800` desktop (`deviceScaleFactor = 1`). All six live checks wait for a tap (`<meta name="checks" content="tap">`) and run on isolated `testStage()` fixtures. Chrome 159 on Android (`A159`) and `npm run check:jank` are **not verified** yet.

This experiment floats one `drawable` capsule dock (`#lt-dock`) over a live scrollable `drawable` HTML feed (`#lt-feed`) inside a single `<canvas id="lt-canvas" content="drawable" layoutsubtree>` backed by one `ElementGL` WebGL 2 context (`/lib/element-gl.js`). A separable 9-tap offscreen Gaussian blur FBO builds the frosted backdrop from `#lt-feed`, unbinds its framebuffer and VAO cleanly before compositing, evaluates the exact analytic gradient of a rounded-capsule SDF to trace per-channel `refract()` along a 20 px bevelled rim, and composites premultiplied `#lt-dock` controls on top at 1:1 CSS pixels.

## Things that bit

- Using an inverse square-root bevel slope `oneMinusT / sqrt(1 - oneMinusT^2)` in the first draft pushed `N.xy` near `0.99` close to the outer edge and cancelled `d(pos)/dy` over a 6 px strip, smearing 1 px body text into tall vertical streaks in dragged screenshots. Switching to a monotonic power curve `1.65 * pow(1.0 - t, 1.15) * depthFactor` with `hSlab = uDepth * 0.74` kept `d(pos)/dy > 0` across the entire bevel so glyphs and stripes curve smoothly into the rim without vertical streaking.
- Tinting `Flat frosted glass` with cool blue `vec3(0.10, 0.13, 0.19)` over a dark navy stripe `#090d16` produced `|R - B| = 13` even with zero refraction, masking the baseline in live check 2. Switching `Flat frosted glass` to a neutral grey tint and testing against `#ffffff` / `#000000` stripes dropped the flat baseline to `|R - B| = 1` vs `116` under `Liquid refraction`.
- Calling `dockEl.setPointerCapture(e.pointerId)` inside `pointerdown` would retarget `pointerup` onto `#lt-dock` and prevent native `click` events on child `<button class="lt-tab">` controls. Deferring `setPointerCapture` until pointer travel exceeds `6 px` preserves native tab clicks while capturing drags smoothly across the canvas.
- Leaving the half-res `BlurPass` framebuffer or `TEXTURE2` active before calling `ElementGL.upload()` risks overwriting the blur texture or drawing into the offscreen FBO. Calling `gl.bindFramebuffer(gl.FRAMEBUFFER, null)`, `gl.bindVertexArray(null)`, and resetting `gl.activeTexture(gl.TEXTURE0)` before `stage.begin()` and `stage.quad()` isolates the passes cleanly.
