## Things that bit

- **VSync `rAF` timestamps predating `performance.now()` on wave launch:** `tick(now)` originally forwarded the `requestAnimationFrame` callback timestamp directly into `stepImpactWaves(..., tMs)`. On the frame right after a `pointerup`, the `rAF` VSync timestamp could sit a few milliseconds earlier than the event's `performance.now()`, so `tMs - wv.startMs < 0` caused the newly launched ring to be skipped on frame zero (`dent 23%, 0/4 rings`). Passing `performance.now()` inside `tick()` and clamping small sub-frame negative deltas (`-50 ms <= rawAgeMs < 0`) to `0` fixed first-frame emission immediately.
- **Avoiding `setPointerCapture()` on interactive children:** calling `setPointerCapture()` on `<canvas>` during `pointerdown` intercepts `pointerup` and breaks button `click` delivery on pad tiles. Tracking the active press on `window` without capturing pointers on `<canvas>` kept `PAD 01`..`PAD 04`, `SW A`/`SW B` and `#ti-label` clickable while still releasing dents cleanly if a finger drags off a tile before lifting.
- **Specular highlight headroom on matte keycaps:** setting specular gain too high pushed bright pressed keycap counters near white (`253` luminance), reducing digit contrast while pressed. Lowering the rim specular coefficient (`0.56` Blinn-Phong + `0.24` rim glow) kept pressed keycap digits crisp while still lifting rim luminance from `212` to `241` over rest.
- **HUD readout clipping at `412 px`:** long route labels (`WebGL 2 ElementGL · GPU dent + wave`) pushed `speed px/s` off the right edge of `.ti-hud` on a phone screen. Shortening the prefix to `WebGL 2 · GPU wave` fits the dent percentage, active ring count and wave speed on one unclipped line at `412 px`.

## For the index

- Transitions, text and motion: `/tactile-impact/` renders a live 3x2 HTML control pad inside one `ElementGL` WebGL 2 canvas that dents under `pointerdown` with concave cavity shading, rim specular highlights and inward pixel pull, then releases up to four concurrent damped concentric elastic shockwaves (`sin(r*k - w*t) * exp(-decay*r - timeDecay*t)`) across neighbouring tiles before settling flat while keeping every button, switch and input clickable (`6/6` live checks pass in Chrome 160 headless; Android phone and `check:jank` not verified).
