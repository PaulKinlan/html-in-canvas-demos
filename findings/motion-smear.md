## Things that bit

- **Padded quad prevents smear and stretch clipping:** drawing `#ms-card` in a tight `cardW x cardH` quad clips any velocity elongation (`stretch > 1`) and directional smear tail at the card border. Expanding the quad by `pad` CSS pixels when `speed >= 0.01` and setting `pad = 0` at rest lets trails bleed freely across the stage grid while keeping settled rendering 1:1 unpadded.
- **Premultiplied alpha bounds across separated RGB taps:** when sampling `sR`, `sG` and `sB` at staggered offsets along velocity, using the average alpha `(ra + ga + ba) / 3` can leave `rgb > alpha` on leading/trailing edges where one channel lands inside the card and the others fall outside. Taking `max(sR.a, max(sG.a, sB.a))` and clamping `rgb <= vec3(alpha)` preserves valid premultiplied edges over the dark stage grid.
- **Software-renderer `dt` clamping and `rAF` timestamp ordering:** clamping `now - state.lastTMs > 100` down to `16 ms` slowed orbit and spring motion roughly 6x under `10 fps` SwiftShader software rendering, and initializing `state.lastTMs = performance.now()` inside a click handler occasionally preceded the first `requestAnimationFrame` timestamp by a few milliseconds (`dt = 0`). Setting `state.lastTMs = null` on triggers and clamping frame gaps at `80 ms` keeps motion responsive on slow frames without teleporting after tab resume.
- **Sizing the 2D fallback buffer up front:** unlike `ElementGL`, calling `canvas.getContext('2d')` leaves the canvas at `300x150` until the first draw. Sizing `canvas.width` and `canvas.height` in `initBackend()` for `?route=2d` avoids an initial `300x150` frame before the first `paint` event.
